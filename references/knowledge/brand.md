# Guide: brand.md

Answers "how we speak". Everything a writer needs to sound like the client. Owned by branding; discovery drafts it from the tone of existing material and what the client says they want, marked as hypotheses.

## Sections

- **Personality:** 3 to 5 traits, each with what it means and what it does not mean ("close, not informal to the point of sloppy").
- **Tone by context:** how the voice shifts across situations (social, sales, support, bad news).
- **Vocabulary:** words and expressions to use, and to avoid for style. Words banned for legal or client reasons live in `company.md` restrictions.
- **Told story:** the origin and the why, as the brand tells it. The facts behind it live in `company.md`.
- **Right and wrong examples:** short pairs showing the voice, preferably real approved pieces. Keep the best few; replace rather than accumulate.

## Does not belong here

What the client stands for strategically (`positioning.md`).

## Example

Good: "Close, not snobbish: talk like someone who knows the producer, not a sommelier. Right: 'This lot came from Seu Antônio's farm, picked in July.' Wrong: 'Notes of red fruits with a velvety body and persistent finish.'"

Bad: "Tone: professional and friendly." (Fits every brand; guides no writing.)
