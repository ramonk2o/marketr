# Guide: marketing.md

Answers "what we do and what worked". The current state of the client's marketing. Changes often: keep `updated` current and date anything time-bound.

## Sections

- **Goals:** each with a date or period and, when possible, a KPI: "Grow subscriptions from ~400 to 600 by 2026-12-31". Mark past-due goals instead of deleting them silently.
- **Budget:** monthly or per-campaign ranges for media and production, if shared.
- **Approvers:** who approves what on the client side, and how (email, WhatsApp, meeting). Typical turnaround.
- **Journey and sales process:** how someone discovers, considers and buys. Who sells, sales cycle, average ticket, where leads get stuck.
- **Channels:** each channel in use with its state: audience size, frequency, what is working, who runs it. Channels tried and abandoned, with why.
- **Tested and results:** campaigns, offers, angles and formats tried, with dates and outcomes. Short; one line each.
- **Recurring seasonality:** dates and periods that matter every year (Black Friday, Mother's Day, harvest, back to school). A specific campaign belongs in its brief, not here.
- **Asset inventory:** what exists and where: photos, videos, logos, testimonials, cases, reviews. Point to the files or links; the content of proof lives in `company.md` or `product.md`.

## Does not belong here

Offers and deadlines of a specific campaign (its brief in `_out/`), who the customers are (`icp.md`).

## Example

Good: "Instagram: ~3k followers, 3 posts a week, run by Júlia. Reels about the producers get the most saves. [hypothesis: team]"

Bad: "We use social media to engage our audience." (No state, no data, nothing to act on.)
