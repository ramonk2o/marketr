# Guide: channels.md

Answers "how we do each channel". The formats agreed with this client, so every piece of a channel comes out the same way without asking again. Owned by copywrite, which fills a channel's section the first time it writes for that channel.

## Sections

One section per channel the client uses, named with the channel id (`email`, `web`, `post`, `video`...). In each, only what was agreed or observed:

- **Format:** length, blocks and their order, number of CTAs, how a piece opens and closes.
- **Conventions:** emojis, hashtags, signature, sender name, capitalization, how prices and dates are written.
- **Tools and constraints:** the email tool and its merge tags, the site builder and its fixed blocks, character limits of a template.
- **Client rules:** what the client asked for or forbade in this channel, with date and source.

Each item says where it came from: inferred from past pieces (`[hypothesis: inferred from _in/<file>]` until the user confirms), agreed with the user, or a client rule.

## Does not belong here

Which channels are in use and how they perform (`marketing.md`), the voice (`brand.md`), platform limits that apply to every client (the channel references of copywrite), anything about one campaign (its brief in `_out/`).

## Example

Good: "Email, promotional: about 150 words, blocks: opening, offer, one button, PS with the deadline. Signed by Ana, founder. No emojis in the subject. Tool: RD Station, merge tag {{lead.name}}, fallback 'Oi'. (agreed 2026-10-30)"

Bad: "Emails should be engaging and well formatted." (Says nothing a writer can follow.)
