# Guide: products/<slug>/product.md

Answers "what we sell", for one product or service. The raw material for any piece that sells it. The slug is the product's name in the client language, lowercase ASCII with hyphens, and is listed in `company.md` under portfolio.

## Sections

- **What it is:** what the customer gets, concretely: format, quantity, duration, delivery.
- **Mechanism:** how it works or why it delivers the result. What makes it work, in terms the customer understands.
- **Transformation:** before and after for the customer. The result they get, not the features.
- **Segment served:** which segment(s) of `icp.md` it serves, by name. If its audience differs entirely, create `products/<slug>/icp.md` instead.
- **Price, format and guarantee:** price, payment options, plans, guarantee, refund policy.
- **How it is bought:** where and how: site, store, WhatsApp, sales call. Steps and friction.
- **Attributes:** features and specs that support the main difference. The main difference itself lives in `positioning.md`.
- **Product proof:** results, numbers, cases, testimonials with their text and source. Real ones only.
- **Answers to objections:** for each objection in `icp.md` that applies, the answer this product gives.

## Does not belong here

Temporary offers and deadlines (a brief in `_out/`), the customer's objections themselves (`icp.md`).

## Example

Good: "Transformation: from supermarket coffee to a café-quality cup at home every morning, without learning to be a barista. [hypothesis: Claude, from website copy]"

Bad: "High-quality product with excellent cost-benefit." (No mechanism, no result, no proof.)
