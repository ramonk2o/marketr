# Guide: company.md

Answers "who we are". Facts about the business, stable over time. Written for the skills that will produce work from it: dense, factual, no marketing prose.

## Sections

- **What it does:** one paragraph a stranger would understand. What they sell, to whom, where.
- **Business model:** how money comes in (direct sales, subscription, services, B2B contracts), sales channels (own site, store, marketplaces, sales team), main revenue sources.
- **Size and stage:** founding year, team size, customers or revenue ranges if shared, stage (new, growing, established, turning around).
- **History:** facts and dates only. The story told as narrative belongs in `brand.md`.
- **Portfolio and offer architecture:** every product or service with its slug and its role (entry offer, core, premium, upsell, bundle), and how they relate. No prices or specs: those live in `products/<slug>/product.md`.
- **Restrictions:** what may never be said or done. Legal and regulatory limits (health, finance, children, alcohol, advertising codes), claims the client forbids, banned words for legal or client reasons. Stylistic dislikes belong in `brand.md`.
- **Institutional proof:** years in the market, number of customers, awards, certifications, notable clients, press. Each with its source. Product results belong in `product.md`.

## Does not belong here

Goals, channels and results (`marketing.md`), audience (`icp.md`), what makes them different (`positioning.md`), tone (`brand.md`).

## Example

Good: "Specialty coffee roaster founded in 2023 in Poços de Caldas (MG). Sells a monthly subscription and a home barista course through its own site and a small shop at the roastery. 2 partners, 6 employees. [hypothesis: inferred from call notes]"

Bad: "A passionate brand that brings the best coffee experience to your cup." (Prose, not facts; nothing a skill can use.)
