# Guide: positioning.md

Answers "why us". The strategic choice of how the client wants to be seen, against the alternatives. Owned by branding; discovery drafts it only from clear statements by the client, or as options the user chose for a new company (logged in `decisions.md`).

## Sections

- **Category:** the frame of reference the customer uses to understand what this is.
- **For whom:** the segment(s) from `icp.md` this positioning targets.
- **Main difference:** the one thing that sets the client apart, which matters to the customer and competitors cannot easily claim. One, not a list.
- **Reason to believe:** the facts that make the difference credible. Point to the proof in `company.md` or `product.md`.
- **Core promise:** what the customer gets, in one sentence.
- **Message pillars:** 3 to 5 themes every communication can draw from, each with a line of support.

## Does not belong here

Product attributes and specs (`product.md`), how to say it (`brand.md`).

## Example

Good: "Main difference: the only roaster that brings each lot's producer story to your door, roasted the same week. Reason to believe: direct trade with 12 Mantiqueira producers (see company.md)."

Bad: "Quality, tradition and innovation." (Any competitor could say it.)
