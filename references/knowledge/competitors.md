# Guide: competitors.md

Answers "against whom". What the customer compares the client with. Owned by research; discovery drafts it with the competitors the client and the team mention and how they see them, marked as hypotheses. When products compete with different players (a course against free videos, a subscription against national clubs), use `products/<slug>/competitors.md`.

## Sections

- **Direct competitors:** 3 to 5, each with name, link, what they offer, price range, offer mechanics that drive the choice (guarantee, shipping, cancellation, trial), the segment they seem to target, the proof they use and where they invest (channels, ads running for months). Short.
- **Alternatives:** what else the customer could do instead: other categories, doing it themselves, doing nothing. Often the real competitor.
- **Comparison:** the purchase criteria customers mention, ranked by evidence (price, freshness, convenience, trust...), and where the client wins and loses against each competitor on them.
- **How they position:** their main promise and tone in a line each, with source (site, ads, profiles).
- **Gaps:** raw material for `positioning.md`. A gap counts only when customers want it (with its evidence level), competitors do not claim it or claim it and fail (their reviews), and the client can deliver it with proof. Label each one "unclaimed" or "claimed but undelivered". Anything short of that is an idea, not a gap.

What competitors' customers praise and complain about goes in the sections above with its evidence level, in the form described in the `icp.md` guide.

## Does not belong here

The client's own differentiation (`positioning.md`).

## Example

Good: "Big subscription clubs (e.g. [missing: names]): cheaper per kilo, ship nationwide, generic messaging about 'gourmet'. Complaints about stale coffee in their reviews (moderate: 4 people, 2 sources). Gap, claimed but undelivered: freshness. The client roasts the week it ships (company.md)."

Bad: "There are many competitors in the market." (Nothing to compare against.)
