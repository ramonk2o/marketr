# Guide: competitors.md

Answers "against whom". What the customer compares the client with. Owned by research; discovery drafts it with the competitors the client and the team mention and how they see them, marked as hypotheses.

## Sections

- **Direct competitors:** each with name, link, what they offer, price range, and how they present themselves. Short.
- **Alternatives:** what else the customer could do instead: other categories, doing it themselves, doing nothing. Often the real competitor.
- **Comparison:** where the client wins and loses against each, on what matters to the customer (price, quality, convenience, trust, service).
- **How they position:** their main promise and tone in a line each, with source (site, ads, profiles).
- **Gaps:** what nobody in the market says or does well. Raw material for `positioning.md`.

## Does not belong here

The client's own differentiation (`positioning.md`).

## Example

Good: "Big subscription clubs (e.g. [missing: names]): cheaper per kilo, ship nationwide, generic messaging about 'gourmet'. Client wins on freshness and producer stories. [hypothesis: client's view]"

Bad: "There are many competitors in the market." (Nothing to compare against.)
